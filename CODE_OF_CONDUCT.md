# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Group 25 is committed to providing an inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone contributing to our DSCI 522 project, Predicting Billboard Hot 100 Top 10 Songs Using Audio Features and Metadata.

We value diversity in backgrounds, identities, experiences, and perspectives. All contributors are expected to treat others with respect, regardless of race, ethnicity, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, religion, age, or level of experience.

## Expected Behavior

All contributors are expected to:

-  Communicate respectfully and professionally.

-  Welcome different perspectives and provide constructive feedback.

-  Collaborate fairly and acknowledge others' contributions.

-  Respect personal boundaries and maintain an inclusive environment.

-  Resolve disagreements respectfully and accept constructive criticism.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

-  Harassment, discrimination, bullying, or intimidation.

-  Offensive comments, personal attacks, or inappropriate language directed at others.

-  Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.

-  Sharing someone's private information without permission.

-  Deliberately disrupting project activities or discussions.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related interactions, including GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, team meetings, and online communications.

## Reporting Violations

Anyone who experiences or witnesses a violation should report it privately to a Group 25 member through Slack.

Reports will be handled promptly, fairly, and as confidentially as reasonably possible. If a report involves a member, contributors may contact another uninvolved member or the DSCI 522 teaching team.

Sensitive incidents should not be reported through public GitHub issues. Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is prohibited.

## Enforcement and Consequences

Group 25 members will review reported incidents, consider the available evidence, and determine appropriate actions based on the severity of the violation.

Consequences may include:

- A private or formal warning.

- Temporary restrictions on project participation.

- Removal of repository access or project participation, where authorized.

- Escalation to the DSCI 522 teaching team or appropriate university authorities for serious or repeated violations.

- Members will aim to enforce these standards consistently and fairly.
